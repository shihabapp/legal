Privacy Policy — Asma (أسماء الله الحسنى)

Last updated: 25 May 2026

Asma ("the app") is a fully offline Android application that displays the 99 Names of Allah. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain terms, how the app handles data.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any personal or device information. It requests no permissions and makes no network connections. It works entirely offline.

Data stored on your device

The only data the app stores is your list of favorite names, saved locally on your device so it is remembered between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
